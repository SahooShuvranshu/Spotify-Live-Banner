## 🔒 Reporting a Vulnerability
If you discover a potential security vulnerability, please do NOT open a public issue. We take security seriously and want to fix it before it's public knowledge.

### How to report:
1.  **Email**: Please send a detailed report to **connect.crystalstudio@gmail.com**.
2.  **Discord**: You can also reach out to the project owner privately via **Discord (Invite: EdbUJHNv9J)**.

### What to include:
- A description of the vulnerability.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- Potential impact.

---

## 🕒 Response Time
We aim to acknowledge receipt of your report within **48 hours** and provide a timeline for fixing the issue as soon as possible.

## 🤝 Our Commitment
We will work with you to resolve the issue promptly and will provide credit for your discovery in our security advisories (unless you prefer to remain anonymous).

Thank you for helping keep the Spotify Live Banner community safe! 🛡️✨
